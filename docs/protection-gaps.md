# Protection gaps and non-goals

- Service-side apply is explicit and inventory-backed. A non-production tenant deployment exercised labels, the custom SIT package and simulation-mode auto-label policies/rules; see [beta notes](../CHANGELOG.md). This does not establish classification accuracy or general tenant readiness.
- Scanner discovery is not equivalent to applying labels. Current source writes label-level conditions through Security & Compliance `Set-Label -Conditions`; scanner cmdlets do not author those mappings. Bootstrap still records `scannerLabelConditionsSupported=false` until independent detection and manual-label behavior validation; tenant configuration alone does not enable enforcement.
- Label publishing is blocked because the documented `New-LabelPolicy` cmdlet has no recipient-user/group audience parameter. The adapter will not publish tenant-wide as a substitute for the configured pilot audience.
- Service auto-label activation requires explicit approval and an operator attestation to a named simulation run. The script verifies inventory ownership and policy/rule configuration before changing mode, but does not independently prove manual-label preservation or actual classification behavior.
- Discovery alone does not prove manual-label preservation or actual protection. Lab enforcement now requires approval tied to native nine-positive BCSI evidence and the exact cluster/UNC; the worker rejects relabeling, downgrades, default-label enforcement, automatic schedules and broader repositories. `Test-ScannerLabelWrites.ps1` separately checks persisted labels and encryption, not proposals. This does not certify every manual-label scenario.
- A label, encryption setting, `Do Not Forward`, or scanner does not replace access control, DLP response, identity governance, incident procedures, backups, or engineering controls. Recipients can photograph, copy, retype, or otherwise disclose viewed information.
- BES group membership is not the only possible source of decryption rights. With automatic owner rights enabled, the Rights Management owner can open independently of the group; service auto-labeling in SharePoint/OneDrive assigns owner/issuer to the last modifier. RMS super users have separate recovery rights. Global Administrator status alone is not an encryption bypass. The lab administrator uploaded the cloud examples, so opening one as that account is not a valid denied-reader test.
- BES grants `VIEW, EDIT, PRINT, EXPORT`, including the right to remove protection. The source adapter does not explicitly manage `DisableAutomaticOwnerRights`; inspect live actions and actual file protection. An empty BES group does not prove that no one can open a protected item. Group changes can also be affected by cached use licenses/offline access.
- Neither scanner metadata verification nor Purview preview failure proves unauthorized-user denial. Encrypted content is not previewable in Content explorer, and portal viewer roles are separate from repository and encryption rights. Use the [owner/member/nonmember access matrix](operations.md#access-test-matrix) and retain separate evidence of persisted encryption and real reader outcomes.
- Limited lab checks of licensing, app consent and client installation are not portable validation. Scan classification accuracy, manual-label preservation and production compatibility remain unverified.
- A portal scanner cluster and assigned content scan job are required for online node configuration. Local cluster settings alone do not prove cloud node visibility. The initial disconnected scan did not validate the later online cluster job.
- The immutable beta.1 assets scan local synthetic files, not the provisioned Azure Files share. Current source adds optional AD DS-authenticated private Azure Files discovery; the lab read all 29 UNC fixtures with zero failures and write-denied scanner access. Native report checks validated all nine BCSI detector/format positives and four negative controls after label policy refresh and the versioned access-pattern correction. This does not establish PII detections, manual-label preservation or actual label application.
- Current discovery enables content inspection while keeping enforcement Off. Before label conditions were deployed, a full reset scan reported General-only proposals and empty sensitive-information/condition columns. Source-defined label conditions and live read-back do not themselves establish native detector behavior; enabling enforcement is not a remedy for missing detections.
- Custom regexes and the synthetic fixture runner are illustrative, not certified detectors. Counts are fictional and count repeated SSN occurrences rather than distinct persons.
- The example makes no NERC CIP compliance determination and does not identify all forms of BCSI. Organizations must have qualified personnel determine applicability, classification, retention, and incident response.

Do not publish labels tenant-wide or enable production automatic labeling until publishing audience, scanner mappings and behavior have been independently reviewed, tested against synthetic edge cases in the target tenant, and explicitly approved. The approved online lab path remains restricted to the dedicated synthetic fixture repository; it does not authorize relabeling or production rollout. See [online lab labeling](operations.md#online-lab-labeling).
